Know what your water is doing before your fish do.

A simple, private log for your freshwater aquarium. Record your tests, keep up with maintenance, and spot trends early. It runs on a Raspberry Pi on your home network.

Built for the way you actually test

Two quick tests a week, a few taps on your phone, and a clear picture of how your tank is doing.

Trend charts

Step-style charts for ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, KH and pH that match how test kits really read.

Maintenance reminders

See at a glance when a test, water change or filter cleaning is due.

Tank journal

Log dosing, livestock additions and incidents so you can connect cause and effect.

How it works

No account, no cloud, and no subscription.

  1. Test your water. Use your usual liquid test and strips.
  2. Log it on your phone. Open the Tankbook page on your home network and enter the results.
  3. Read the dashboard. Charts, alerts and reminders update instantly.
Tankbook is a personal side project. Runs on a Raspberry Pi 4 with Python and Flask.
