Hi, I'm Denver Chernin.

I'm a software engineer who loves fish tanks, and I built Tankbook to finally keep mine in good shape.

My story

I write software for a living, and in my spare time I keep freshwater aquariums. I love them, but I've struggled to maintain a healthy tank consistently. Water tests get skipped, water changes slip a few days, and when something goes wrong I can't remember what changed.

I decided to combine my two interests: build a tool that fits the way I actually test and take care of my tank.

Why I made Tankbook

Tankbook is a way to keep track of my tank and hold myself accountable. A written record turns "I think it was fine last week" into numbers I can look at, and reminders mean maintenance doesn't depend on my memory.

Keep track

Log my water tests so I can see what's really happening over time.

Stay accountable

Reminders for tests, water changes and filter cleaning keep me on schedule.

Learn from it

Notes on dosing, livestock and incidents help me connect cause and effect.

What it's built with

Tankbook is a personal project running on a Raspberry Pi on my home network.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • Raspberry Pi
